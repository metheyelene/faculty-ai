Acadora

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Acadora ("the app") is a personal academic assistant for faculty. This policy explains what data the app handles and how. The short version: all of your data stays on your device.

1. What data the app stores

  • Profile information — your name, preferred name, designation and department, entered by you during onboarding.
  • Schedule data — timetable class slots you create, or import from a timetable photo.
  • Attendance records — student rosters and per-session present/absent/leave marks you record.
  • Tasks and notes — reminders you create and notes you write.
  • Saved memories — facts you explicitly choose to save for the assistant.

2. Where the data lives

All data is stored only on your device, in the app's private database and preferences. The app works fully offline and has no servers, no accounts and no analytics.

3. What leaves your device

Nothing. The app does not transmit your data anywhere:

  • No analytics or crash-reporting services are integrated.
  • No advertising SDKs are present.
  • Timetable photo import uses an on-device text recognition model; the photo is processed locally and never uploaded.
  • Notifications are generated locally by your device's alarm and notification systems.

4. Permissions the app uses

  • Notifications — to show task reminders and class alerts you schedule.
  • Alarms — to deliver reminders at exact times, restored automatically after reboot.
  • Camera — only when you choose to import a timetable photo; images stay on the device.

5. Data retention and deletion

Your data is kept until you remove it. You can delete data at any time:

  • Individually — delete a task, note, memory or attendance session inside the app.
  • Completely — uninstall the app, or use Settings → Reset demo data.

6. Children's privacy

Acadora is designed for adult users (faculty). It is not directed at children under 13, does not knowingly collect their data, and no data is collected from any user because nothing leaves the device.

7. Changes to this policy

If a future version adds optional cloud sync or AI services, this policy will be updated before those features ship, and the Play Store data safety form will be revised accordingly.
